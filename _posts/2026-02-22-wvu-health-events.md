Part of the <a href="/wvu-health-design-system/">WVU Health design system</a>, an engaging, scalable, and responsive approach to displaying event information covers both a browsable listing and individual detail pages, each requiring close attention to hierarchy, scannability, and brand consistency across desktop and mobile.

The listing surfaces critical information at a glance, while detail pages provide a deeper, structured view for attendees seeking schedules, registration, and accreditation details.
