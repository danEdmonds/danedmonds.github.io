In the <a href="/design-system/">WVU Health design system</a>, the flow-based rich text editor supports an editorial approach to content creation, allowing marketing-focused editors, primarily school communicators, to write naturally while inserting richer components seamlessly within the flow, without managing rigid layouts or unnecessary blocks. This keeps editing intuitive and efficient, especially for text-heavy pages, and maintains consistency across the site.
