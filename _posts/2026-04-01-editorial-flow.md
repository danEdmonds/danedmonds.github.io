This flow-based rich text editor supports an editorial approach to content creation, allowing marketing-focused editors, primarily school communicators, to write naturally while inserting richer components seamlessly within the flow, without managing rigid layouts or unnecessary blocks. As part of the <a href="/design-system/">WVU Health design system</a>, this keeps editing intuitive and efficient, especially for text-heavy pages, and maintains consistency across the site.
